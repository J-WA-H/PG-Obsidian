Get into habit of reading arXiv every day

Look up supervisor on INSPIRE and who has cited them

Reading papers: abstract, intro, then conclusions

Find data from PDG, HEPdata

Treat PhD like a job:
- Pick some fixed work hours and try to stick with them, but its flexible so go home if unproductive or stay late if very productive
- Should be able get everything done in working hours without working evenings/weekends.
- If you get offered two or three projects take them

Think about frequency of supervisor meetings
