For a collinear factorisation, cross-sections for the hadronic production of an $n$-parton final state in a reaction of the type $h_{1}h_{2}\to n+X$ with hadrons $h_{1}$ and $h_{2}$ in the initial state can be written as
$$\sigma_{2\to n} = \sum_{a,b}\int_{0}^{1} dx_{a}dx_{b}f_{a/h_{1}}(x_{a},\mu_{F})f_{b/h_{2}}(x_{b},\mu_{F})\hat{\sigma}_{ab\to n}(\mu_{F},\mu_{R})=\sum_{a,b}\int_{0}^{1} dx_{a}dx_{b} f_{a/h_{1}}(x_{a},\mu_{F})f_{b/h_{2}}(x_{b},\mu_{F}) \frac{1}{2\hat{s}}\int d\Phi_{n}|\mathcal{M}_{ab\to n}|^2(\Phi_{n};\mu_{F},\mu_{R})\tag{7.1}.$$
A number of objects enters this master formula, namely:
- The $f_{a/h}(x,\mu)$ denote the parton distribution functions (PDFs), which depend on  the light-cone momenta fractions $x$ parton $a$ has with respect to the hadron $h,$ which incorporates it, and on the factorisation scale $\mu_{F}$. The $x$ also connects the centre of mass energies squared of the hadronic collision $E_{c.m.}^{2}(h_{1}h_{2})^2=s$ with its partonic counterpart $E^2_{c.m} (ab)=\hat{s}$ through
$$\hat{s}=x_{a}x_{b}s\tag{7.2}.$$
	At leading order, $f_{a/h}(x,\mu)$ can be interpreted as probabilities to find $a$ in $h$ with momentum fraction $x$ at the space-like resolution scale $\mu$ - an interpretation deeply connected to the case of deep-inelastic scattering, where factorisation has been proven and where PDFs have traditionally been defined and measured. With this probabilistic interpretation at hand, the integral over the momentum fractions $x$ is well understood.
- The parton-level cross-section $d\hat{\sigma}_{ab\to n}(\mu_{F},\mu_{R})$ which is given by the product of the incoming partonic flux
$$\frac{1}{4\sqrt{ (p_{a}\cdot p_{b})^2 - p_{a}^{2}p_{b}^{2} }}\xrightarrow{m_{a,b}\to 0} \frac{1}{2\hat{s}}=\frac{1}{2x_{a}x_{b}s}\tag{7.3}$$
	of the massless partons $a,b$ and the integral of the partonic transition amplitude squared, $|\mathcal{M}_{ab\to n}|^{2}(\Phi_{n};\mu_{F},\mu_{R})$, over the available n-parton phase space element $d\Phi_{n}$. This phase-space element is given by
$$d\Phi_{n}=\prod_{i=1}^{n}\left[ \frac{dp_{i}}{(2\pi)^{4}}(2\pi)\delta(p_{i}^{2}-m_{i}^{2})\Theta(p_{i}^{(0)}) \right](2\pi)^{4}\delta^{(4)} \left( p_{a}+p_{b}-\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i} \right)\tag{7.4}.$$
	Each term in the square brackets is the Lorentz invariant phase space element of a particle $i$ with momentum $p_{i}$ and mass $m_{i}$. The additional $\delta$-function at the end implements total 4-momentum conservation.
- The factorisation scale $\mu_{F}$ and the renormalisation scale $\mu_{R}$ are process-dependent quantities. The former, $\mu_{F}$ signifies the scale at which the parton distribution functions are determined, while the latter, $\mu_{R}$ indicates the scale at which the coupling constants are evaluated, thus taking into account the quantum nature of the underlying theory. Typically for simple processes which are characterised by one scale only, $\mu_{F}$ and $\mu_{R}$ as assumed to be the same.
