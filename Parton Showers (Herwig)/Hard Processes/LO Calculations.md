The LO cross section $\sigma^{LO}[u]$ is given by integrals over longitudinal momentum fractions $x_{a},x_{b}$ of two incoming initial state partons, and the Lorentz invariant phase space $\phi_{n}$, where $n$ is the number of outgoing final state partons. For two incoming hadrons $A,B$, weighted by an observable $u$,
$$\sigma^{LO}[u]=\int df\,d\sigma^{B}(\phi_{n})u(\phi_{n})\tag{1.1} = \sum_{a,b}\sum_{\{ n|ab \}} \int df_{ab}(x_{a},x_{b})d\sigma^B_{ab}(\phi_{n|ab}|p_{a}(x_{a}),p_{b}(x_{b}))u(\phi_{n|ab}|p_{a}(x_{a}),p_{b}(x_{b}))$$
where we sum over all possible partonic configurations, also referred to as the hard processes or subprocesses, $a,b\to f_{n}=\{ n|ab \}$, that can occur in the hadronic scattering. From conservation of momentum, $p_{a}(x_{a})+p_{b}(x_{b})=x_{a}P_{A}+x_{b}P_{B}=Q=p_{1}+p_{2}+\dots+p_{n}$, we know that the total momentum $Q$ is implicitly dependent on the longitudinal momentum fractions. Here $P_{A},P_{B}$ are the incoming hadron momenta, i.e. $\phi_{n}=\{ \phi_{n|ab}|p_{a}(x_{a}),p_{b}(x_{b}) \}=\{ \phi_{n|ab}|x_{a}P_{A},x_{b}P_{B} \}$.

The differential cross section for the subprocess $a,b\to f_{n}=\{ n|ab \}$ is given by the Born matrix element squared, weighted by the parton distribution functions for the incoming partons and the flux factors,
$$\frac{df_{ab}d\sigma_{ab}^B}{d\phi_{n|ab}dx_{a}dx_{b}}=\frac{\hbar^2c^2}{\mathcal{F}(p_{a}(x_{a})p_{b}(x_{b}))}f_{a/A}(x_{a},\mu_{F})\mathcal{B}_{n|ab}(\phi_{n|ab}|p_{a}(x_{a})p_{b}(x_{b}))\theta_{c}(\phi_{n|ab}|p_{a}(x_{a})p_{b}(x_{b}))\tag{1.2}$$and where the Born matrix element squared is essentially evaluated as the square of a vector in spin and colour space $\braket{ \mathcal{M}^{(0)} | \mathcal{M}^{(0)} }$,
$$\mathcal{B}_{n|ab}(\phi_{n|ab}|p_{a}(x_{a})p_{b}(x_{b}))=N_{n|ab}\braket{ \mathcal{M}_{n|ab}^{(0)}(\phi_{n|ab}|p_{a}(x_{a}),p_{b}(x_{b})) |  \mathcal{M}_{n|ab}^{(0)}(\phi_{n|ab}|p_{a}(x_{a}),p_{b}(x_{b}))}\tag{1.3}, $$
with an additional weight $N_{n|ab}=1/(S_{n|ab}n_{s}(a,b)n_{c}(a,b))$ containing the symmetry factors for identical final-state partons and averaging factors for initial state spin and colour degrees of freedom. The default convention for amplitudes in [Matchbox](Hard%20Processes) is that all physical processes are calculated by crossing an amplitude with all legs outgoing to the reaction under consideration.

The flux factor $\mathcal{F}(p_{a}p_{b})=4p_{a}p_{b}=2x_{a}x_{b}S$, where $S$ is the hadronic centre of mass energy.
