The idea of recycling equivalent parts of [[LO Matrix Elements]] can be achieved via recursion relations. The basic idea is to create one-particle off-shell parts of the amplitude recursively. The idea in all methods is to construct generalised currents $\mathcal{J}_{\alpha}(\pi)$ for a set $\pi$ of external particles on their mass shell plus one internal one, which can be off-shell. In this construction, $\alpha$ denotes the combined quantum numbers of this internal particle, including spin, colour, e.t.c.. The external particle may also be considered as currents; then the quantum numbers $\alpha$ are directly identified with the ones of this particle. From these special cases, recursively more and more complex currents are built by applying
$$\mathcal{J}_{\alpha}(\pi)=P_{\alpha}(\pi)\left\{  \sum_{\mathcal{P}_{2}(\pi)}\sum_{\mathcal{V}_{\alpha}^{\alpha_{1}\alpha_{2}}} [S(\pi_{1},\pi_{2})\mathcal{V}_{\alpha}^{\alpha_{1}\alpha_{2}}\mathcal{J}_{\alpha_{1}}(\pi_{1})\mathcal{J}_{\alpha_{2}}(\pi_{2})]+\sum_{\mathcal{P}_{3}(\pi)}\sum_{\mathcal{V}_{\alpha}^{\alpha_{1}\alpha_{2}\alpha_{3}}}[S(\pi_{1},\pi_{2},\pi_{3})\mathcal{V}_{\alpha}^{\alpha_{1}\alpha_{2}\alpha_{3}}\mathcal{J}_{\alpha_{1}}(\pi_{1})\mathcal{J}_{\alpha_{2}}(\pi_{2})\mathcal{J}_{\alpha_{3}}(\pi_{3})] \right\}\tag{5.1}.$$
Here $P_{\alpha}(\pi)$ is the propagator denominator, which depends on the properties $\alpha$ of the propagating particle and on its momentum given by the momenta in $\pi$. The terms $S(\pi_{1},\pi_{2})$ and $S(\pi_{1},\pi_{2},\pi_{3})$ take care of symmetry factors, which emerge in the partitions $\mathcal{P}_{2}(\pi):\pi\to \pi_{1}\oplus \pi_{2}$ and $\mathcal{P}_{2}(\pi):\pi\to \pi_{1}\oplus \pi_{2}\oplus \pi_{3}$ of the original set. Finally the $\mathcal{V}_{\alpha}$ signify the three and four leg vertices connecting the particles $\alpha_{i}$ of the sub-currents with the emerging new particle $\alpha$. This allows us to write the total amplitude $\mathcal{A}(\pi)$ for one specific configuration of $\pi$ as
$$\mathcal{A}(\pi)=\mathcal{J}_{\alpha_{\rho}}(\rho) \frac{1}{P_{\bar{\alpha}_{\rho}}(\pi|\rho)}\bar{\alpha}_{\textbackslash}(\pi|\rho)\tag{5.2},$$
where $\pi|\rho$ denotes the residual subset of $\pi$ after the subset $\rho$ has been taken off. Overall conservation of quantum numbers also guarantees that the combined quantum number $\alpha_{\pi|\rho}$ of this conjugate subset is the conjugate of the set $\rho,\bar{\alpha}_{\rho}$. It is worth noting that it is computationally advantageous to map the four vertices onto vertices with three legs only.

For some specific cases it is possible to solve the recursion equation in closed form. For instance a current with $n$ external colour-ordered like-sign gluons is given by
$$J^{\mu}(1^{+},2^{+},\dots,n^{+})=g^{n-2}_{s} \frac{\bra{q^{-}}\gamma^{\mu}\not\!P_{1,n}\ket{q^{-}}}{\sqrt{ 2 }\langle q 1 \rangle \langle 12 \rangle \langle 23 \rangle \dots \langle (n-1)n \rangle \langle nq \rangle  },\tag{5.3}  $$
where four momentum $P_{i,j}^{\mu}$ is constructed from the outgoing momenta through
$$P_{i,j}^{\mu}=\sum_{l=1}^{j-1}p_{l}^{\mu},\tag{5.4}$$
and where $q^{\mu}$ is the gauge vector. This current can be used to prove the form of the maximally helicity violating (MHV) or Parke-Taylor amplitudes, which correspond to all gluonic, colour-ordered amplitudes with all equal signs apart from two, labelled $i,j$ in the equation below,
$$\mathcal{A}(1^{+},2^{+},\dots, i^{-},\dots,j^{-},\dots,n^{+})=ig_{s}^{n-2} \frac{{\langle ij \rangle ^{4}}}{\langle 12 \rangle \langle 23 \rangle \dots \langle (n-1)n \rangle \langle n 1 \rangle }\\ \mathcal{A}(1^{-},2^{-},\dots,i^{+},\dots,j^{+},\dots,n^{-})=ig_{s}^{n-2} \frac{[ij]^{4}}{[12][23]\dots[(n-1)n][n 1]}.\tag{5.5}$$
Note that all-sign identical amplitudes vanish due to the conservation of angular momentum.
